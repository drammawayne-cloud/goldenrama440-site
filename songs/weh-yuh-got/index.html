Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Weh Yuh Got opens its exchange as a challenge, with Golden Rama’s narrator directing attention toward an adult encounter. The question in the title gives the performance an immediate point of contact between the speaker and the person being addressed.

The language is sexually explicit, while the wider scene is one of attraction and confident invitation. Its directness is central to the characterization, leaving the listener in little doubt about the provocative mood of the record.

The appeal of the title is how quickly it starts a conversation. In the context of the collection, this song adds another form of playful address, using a short question to organize a performance built on attitude and response.
