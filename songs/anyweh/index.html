Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

AnyWeh makes reach and persistence the central ideas of a confrontational performance. Golden Rama’s narrator presents a threat that recognizes no fixed location, giving the repeated title an insistent and deliberately unsettling meaning.

The writing moves through a series of charged images while returning to the same claim of range. That structure makes the word feel less like a destination than a declaration of the character’s supposed ability to appear wherever he chooses.

This selection belongs to the harder dramatic material in the catalog. Its distinguishing quality is the emphasis on movement without limits, turning the title into the idea that holds the entire performance’s threatening atmosphere together.
