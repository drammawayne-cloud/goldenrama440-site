Select It

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Select It belongs to the darker, more confrontational part of Golden Rama’s catalog. Its performance uses sharp threats and stark imagery to establish a character whose speech is immediate, forceful and unwilling to soften its impact.

The brief title matches that direct approach. The writing presses forward through a succession of challenges, creating tension through verbal insistence and the sense that each line must land with a decisive effect.

As a listening experience, this record is defined by intensity rather than reassurance. It shows a dramatic side of Golden Rama’s writing, where the strength of the persona and the pressure of its language shape the mood.
