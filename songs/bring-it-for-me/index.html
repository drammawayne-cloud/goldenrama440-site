Bring It For Me

Shop this release

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Bring It For Me organizes its performance around a direct, repeated invitation. Golden Rama’s narrator addresses a partner in an explicitly adult setting, making desire immediate and personal through the insistence of the central phrase.

The repetition gives the song a recognizable point of return. Instead of developing an elaborate narrative, the voice remains close to the encounter, allowing a short request and the attitude behind it to carry the performance.

The title illustrates how a simple piece of everyday language can become a strong musical cue. In this recording, that cue takes on a provocative character, adding another direct form of address to Golden Rama’s mature party material.
