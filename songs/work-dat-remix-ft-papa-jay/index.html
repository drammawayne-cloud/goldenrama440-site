Work Dat - Remix Ft - Papa Jay

Membership streaming

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Work Dat puts movement in the foreground, and this remix keeps Papa Jay’s featured contribution attached to the title. The record’s repeated instruction is easy to grasp, giving the audience a straightforward way into its party-minded performance.

The central phrase functions as an invitation to participate rather than an elaborate story. Its insistence creates a performance space in which a short idea can keep returning, gathering its meaning from the people responding to it.

The remix label identifies the specific version presented here and is worth preserving when sharing the song. Within the catalog, this release contributes to the direct, adult dance material that makes Golden Rama’s collection varied in mood and purpose.
