Divide & Conquer

Membership streaming

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Divide & Conquer points toward power, division and the messages people are asked to accept. References to politics and public information give Golden Rama’s performance a questioning stance, bringing a social concern into the wider collection.

The title names a strategy in which separation serves someone else’s advantage. As a lens for the song, it encourages the listener to consider who benefits from conflict and how distrust can shape the way a community sees itself.

This selection adds a different kind of tension to the catalog. The challenge is directed toward influence and authority, giving the listener room to think about the relationship between personal conviction, public narratives and collective strength.
