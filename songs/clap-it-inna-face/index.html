Clap It Inna Face

Membership streaming

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Clap It Inna Face announces a confrontational mood and follows through with a deliberately fierce performance. The writing uses threats and dramatic imagery to create a character whose defining quality is an almost unbroken pressure of attack.

The language is designed for force, with each phrase contributing to the same charged atmosphere. Its impact comes from the accumulation of attitude, presenting a musical persona that insists on being heard without seeking a softer introduction.

This recording belongs to the most aggressive corner of Golden Rama’s range. Considered alongside his romantic, spiritual and celebratory work, it demonstrates how far the catalog can move between different characters and emotional registers.
