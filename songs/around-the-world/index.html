Around The World

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Around The World uses travel as the frame for an adult, flirtatious performance. Golden Rama’s narrator moves through an imagined geography of encounters, turning distance and variety into part of the song’s confident personality.

The references to different places expand the scale of the story without changing its central interest in attraction. The world becomes a stage for the character, whose attention remains on the people he imagines meeting along the way.

This is a performance of possibility and appetite, rather than a travel diary. Its place in the collection is among the playful, mature releases, with the title giving that familiar persona a wider horizon to occupy.
