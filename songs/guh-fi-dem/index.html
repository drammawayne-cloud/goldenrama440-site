Guh Fi Dem

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Guh Fi Dem, credited to Shane P and Golden Rama, presents a confrontational performance with a deliberately hard edge. The language of challenge and retaliation gives the recording a high-pressure atmosphere, establishing its combative character from the outset.

The title works as a short command, matching the directness of that voice. Rather than a reflective account, the song offers a charged performance in which attitude and verbal impact are central to the effect.

This is one of the catalog’s fiercer collaborations, and the shared artist credit matters to how it is presented. Read as a piece of musical characterization, it adds intensity and a sharply drawn persona to Golden Rama’s wider body of releases.
