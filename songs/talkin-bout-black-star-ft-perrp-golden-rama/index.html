Talkin Bout-Black Star Ft Perrp & Golden Rama

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Talkin Bout carries Black Star, Perrp and Golden Rama in its release credit and centers on a familiar claim of reputation: being the person everyone is discussing. The performance delivers that claim with a confrontational edge and an insistence on recognition.

The recurring statement gives the record a clear focal point. Around it, the language of defiance builds a larger-than-life character, making public attention feel like both a reward and a challenge to anyone questioning the speaker.

This is a useful example of Golden Rama’s place within a collaborative release. The track belongs to the combined credit, and its hard-edged identity comes through as a shared presentation of confidence rather than a quiet personal reflection.
