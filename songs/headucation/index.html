Headucation

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Headucation uses a playful title and a staged invitation to perform as the opening for adult wordplay. Golden Rama’s narrator turns the language of singing, the microphone and being judged into part of a provocative comic scenario.

That overlap between musical performance and double meaning gives the recording its particular identity. The humor depends on recognizing that the familiar terms are being used to suggest another conversation beneath the one apparently taking place.

This selection shows a theatrical side of the catalog’s mature material. Its character comes from the setup, the repeated imagery and the voice’s confidence in the joke, making wordplay as important to the experience as the invitation itself.
