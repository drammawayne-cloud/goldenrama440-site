Inna Dem Head Ft Hollow Point

Shop this release

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Inna Dem Head brings Golden Rama and Hollow Point together in a fiercely confrontational performance. The writing is dominated by threats and graphic dramatic imagery, giving the record a dark identity that extends well beyond the suggestion of its title.

The recurring lines reinforce the same combative character, using insistence to keep the pressure high. This is a performance built for intensity, with the voice remaining inside its chosen persona rather than moving toward a reflective explanation.

Hollow Point’s featured credit is part of the collaboration’s identity and should travel with the song. Heard within the wider catalog, the release marks one of its harder extremes, contrasting sharply with Golden Rama’s expressions of affection, gratitude and purpose elsewhere.
