Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Gud Gud centers on adult attraction and emphatic praise. Golden Rama’s narrator uses repetition to communicate pleasure and admiration, turning the doubled word in the title into a direct expression of how strongly the moment is being felt.

The language is sexually explicit, while the emotional frame includes the satisfaction of being with the person addressed. That combination gives the performance an intimate scale even when the delivery remains bold and outgoing.

The simplicity of the title is part of its character. Rather than explaining the feeling at length, the record keeps returning to an uncomplicated judgment, allowing insistence and attitude to carry its mature, flirtatious appeal.
