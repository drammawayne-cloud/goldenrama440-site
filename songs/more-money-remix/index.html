More Money-Remix

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

More Money-Remix puts ambition directly in its name. Its opening spirit is one of forward movement and refusal to be held back, giving the pursuit of more a determined character rather than presenting it as a distant wish.

The remix designation matters because it identifies this particular version of the record. Listeners exploring Golden Rama’s releases can hear it as another presentation of a familiar ambition, while keeping the published version title intact.

Placed beside the catalog’s other images of wealth and achievement, this selection contributes to a larger conversation about wanting progress. Its appeal is in the urgency of that desire and the confidence of a performer unwilling to stand still.
