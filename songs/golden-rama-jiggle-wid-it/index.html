Golden Rama - Jiggle Wid It

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Jiggle Wid It gives its audience a movement to follow from the title onward. Golden Rama builds the performance around dancing, playful address and a strong invitation to show personality, making participation a central part of the song’s appeal.

The repeated movement cues keep the record focused on the body in motion. Its language is lively and adult, carrying the exaggerated confidence of a party performance rather than the careful detail of a relationship narrative.

This selection fits the dance-oriented side of Golden Rama’s creative identity. It is the kind of title that carries an instruction as well as a name, giving listeners something to do together when the recording comes on.
