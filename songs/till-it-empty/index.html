Till It Empty

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Till It Empty takes a severe, confrontational stance and sustains it through a repeated central idea. Golden Rama’s performance works with the language of threat, giving the recording a relentless quality that is announced by the title itself.

Repetition is important to how the character comes across. The returning phrase provides little sense of retreat, making insistence part of the dramatic effect and keeping the record focused on its uncompromising attitude.

Within the collection, this is one of the selections that foregrounds a hard-edged performance persona. Its purpose is impact: a concentrated expression of tension whose force comes from how firmly the voice occupies its chosen mood.
