Golden Rama & Claud Matteo-Want U

Shop this release

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Want U brings Golden Rama and Claud Matteo together around a direct expression of attraction. The central declaration is deliberately uncomplicated, giving the listener an immediate emotional point of entry before any larger interpretation is needed.

That simplicity makes space for the feeling of the performance. A song built around wanting someone can communicate through repetition and emphasis, allowing the same thought to return with the insistence of an emotion that will not settle.

Within the collaboration section, this release offers a softer point of contrast to the more confrontational titles. The two-artist credit gives it a shared identity, while its straightforward romantic address keeps the focus on a recognizable human desire for connection.
