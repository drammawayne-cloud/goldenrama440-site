Zombie

Membership streaming

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Zombie brings horror imagery into Golden Rama’s confrontational writing. The title supplies an unsettling figure through which the performance can express menace, creating a character whose force seems to come from being difficult to slow or stop.

The record combines that image with threats and geographic references, widening its dramatic setting. The result is a darker piece of musical storytelling, where the atmosphere depends on the speaker’s exaggerated power and persistence.

The horror element gives this selection a particular identity within the harder releases. It asks to be heard through the imagination of the performance, with the title acting as the image that holds its threatening mood together.
