Spend It Wid You

Shop this release

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Spend It Wid You connects companionship with ambition, asking what a better future might mean when it is shared. Golden Rama’s narrator values a partner whose company suits his energy, giving the title a personal purpose beyond the idea of spending.

The writing brings money and integrity into the same conversation. Wanting progress does not erase the desire to remain honest about one’s values, and that tension gives the relationship at the center a wider context.

For me, this is a record about the company we choose while building a life. Its strongest idea is that achievement and connection do not have to compete: the person beside the ambition can be part of what makes the ambition matter.
