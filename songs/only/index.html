Only

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Only is a compact title with an expansive set of possibilities: independence, exclusivity and the desire to be the person who matters most. Golden Rama’s confident performance brings those ideas into an adult world of self-possession and attraction.

The recurring emphasis on being one’s own person sits alongside the attention of others. That combination gives the record an interesting push and pull, between standing apart and enjoying the recognition that draws people closer.

I hear the title as a question of value as much as a declaration of confidence. In the wider catalog, Only creates room to consider what it means to claim a position for yourself while still wanting to be chosen.
