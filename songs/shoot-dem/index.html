Shoot Dem

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Shoot Dem is a forceful piece of musical characterization, built around a combative voice and a repeated threatening command. Golden Rama uses stark imagery to create a dark setting in which tension is meant to remain close to the surface.

The succession of dramatic references and challenges gives the performance its momentum. The record does not rely on a long introduction to establish its identity; the title and the voice move quickly into the same severe mood.

Its place in the catalog is as an uncompromising expression of the hard-edged persona. The contrast with Golden Rama’s more hopeful and reflective selections helps show the breadth of roles an artist can inhabit across different recordings.
