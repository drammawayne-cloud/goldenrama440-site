Alien Brain

Membership streaming

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Alien Brain makes difference a point of pride. Golden Rama’s performance draws attention to mental sharpness, verbal skill and the ambition to operate beyond the ordinary, turning the title’s otherworldly image into a statement about artistic self-belief.

The references to changing the flow and reaching beyond familiar surroundings strengthen that reading. The character being presented is alert to technique as well as status, treating the ability to surprise listeners as part of what sets him apart.

In a catalog rich with distinctive personas, this release gives imagination a central role. Alien Brain suggests a mind refusing to stay inside an expected pattern, and its confident language makes that refusal part of the performance itself.
