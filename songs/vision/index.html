Vision

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Vision uses the language of sight and precision inside a confrontational performance. Golden Rama presents an alert, combative character, making awareness and certainty part of the recording’s attitude rather than treating the title as a gentle reflection.

The imagery is forceful, and the voice works to establish control of the situation it describes. That gives the song a distinct reading of vision: seeing clearly becomes part of a performance about confidence, readiness and competitive presence.

The title adds another dimension to the harder material in the collection. It brings a mental quality into the dramatic persona, connecting outward force with the character’s claim to focus and a sharply defined sense of intention.
