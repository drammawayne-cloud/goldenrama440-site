A Chance

Membership streaming

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

A Chance places attraction inside the immediate setting of a dance. The central request is personal and adult, turning a brief opportunity for connection into the focus of Golden Rama’s confident, flirtatious performance.

The repeated appeal gives the record a clear point of return. Its sense of possibility comes from the moment itself: the approach, the response and the anticipation of whether that invitation will lead anywhere.

The title carries a familiar human wish, even within the song’s provocative language. It is the wish to be noticed and given an opening, expressed here through the outgoing persona and social setting of a party record.
