Integrity

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Integrity places purpose, motivation and earned achievement at the heart of its performance. Golden Rama’s writing emphasizes working for what one has and moving with intention, giving confidence a foundation in effort rather than appearance alone.

The title sets a useful standard for those ideas. Ambition becomes more meaningful when connected to consistency, and the record’s forward-looking attitude gives the listener a sense of a person trying to match his position with his principles.

This is a strong companion to Real Richhh in the wider collection. Both titles make room for a discussion of value beyond display, while Integrity brings the emphasis back to the discipline and character required to keep moving toward a goal.
