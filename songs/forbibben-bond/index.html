Forbibben Bond

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Forbibben Bond places the idea of an uneasy connection at the center of its title. A bond can offer closeness and belonging, while the suggestion of a boundary immediately complicates what that connection might mean for the people involved.

The brief spoken exchange in the opening adds a dramatic quality to that starting point. It encourages attention to character and implication, leaving the listener to consider what is being understood between the voices and what remains outside the conversation.

My reading of this title is concerned with the tension between attachment and limits. Within Golden Rama’s collection, it opens a more questioning space, where the meaning of a relationship can be as important as the strength of its attraction.
