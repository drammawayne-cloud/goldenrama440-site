Live Inna It

Membership streaming

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Live Inna It uses an insistent refrain to establish an openly adult, provocative mood. Golden Rama’s performance works through sexual wordplay and direct address, giving the song the exaggerated confidence of an intimate boast made for a party audience.

The repeated title makes a short phrase carry much of the record’s personality. Rather than developing a complicated story, the performance returns to a central image and lets the surrounding language intensify its meaning.

This selection belongs to the mature, flirtatious part of the collection. Its character comes from a deliberately bold delivery of desire, adding another distinct mood beside Golden Rama’s recordings concerned with faith, reputation and ambition.
