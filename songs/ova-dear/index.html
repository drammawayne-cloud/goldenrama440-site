Ova Dear

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Ova Dear is organized around a repeated refusal to cross into someone else’s space. Golden Rama’s narrator defines his position by drawing a boundary, making rejection and group identity the most visible ideas in the performance.

The lyric uses ridicule and derogatory references to sexuality as part of that boundary-making. Those choices give the song a confrontational and exclusionary tone, and they are part of what a careful reading of the record needs to acknowledge.

For me, the title opens a question about how belonging is expressed through distance from others. This is a recording to consider critically as well as musically, paying attention to what the repeated refusal reveals about the voice speaking it.
