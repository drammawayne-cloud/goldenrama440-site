Plaqes & Racks - MixTape Intro

Shop this release

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Plaqes & Racks opens with an introduction that announces the project as an experience to enter. Dramatic spoken material and an announcing voice establish a theatrical frame, giving the listener a sense of arrival before the wider collection unfolds.

An intro has a different job from a conventional single. It can set a scale, establish an attitude and suggest how the following recordings belong together, allowing the project’s identity to take shape in a short space.

This opening matters as part of Golden Rama’s catalog because a body of work is more than its individual highlights. Plaqes & Racks - MixTape Intro serves as the doorway, inviting the listener to hear the collection with a sense of sequence and occasion.
