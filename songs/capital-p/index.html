Capital P

Membership streaming

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Capital P enters the collection as an adult dance record, with direct movement cues and an admiring address to its central figure. Golden Rama’s performance puts physical expression in the foreground, inviting the listener into a lively exchange of attention.

The repeated invitation gives the recording a clear organizing idea. Its language is playful and provocative, allowing a short phrase to become a point of response for the person being addressed and for the audience around them.

This selection contributes to the catalog’s party atmosphere through confidence and repetition. Its character is outward and participatory, built for a moment in which a familiar cue can connect the performer, the dancer and the wider room.
