Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Cockey leans into a deliberately adult form of self-confidence, with Golden Rama’s narrator describing the attention he receives and the impression he makes. The title participates in that swagger, carrying a provocative double meaning into the performance.

The writing presents attraction as part of the character’s public identity. People notice, follow and respond to him, and the voice treats that response as confirmation of the image it is determined to project.

This is a character built through exaggeration and attitude, not a quiet declaration of affection. The recording adds to the catalog’s playful, mature side by making the performer’s confidence itself the subject of the scene.
