Golden Rama Radio privacy

Golden Rama Radio is published by Dwayne Vidal / Rich Row Records. This policy covers the iPhone, iPad and Android radio app.

Updated November 25, 2026

Your collection stays on your device

No account is required to listen to the radio or browse public content. Saved song bookmarks and cached station history are stored locally. We do not upload your bookmarks or searches to a user profile. Saved songs are bookmarks, not music downloads.

Information used to deliver radio

When you listen or request song information and artwork, the radio service receives the network information necessary to respond. This can include your IP address, requested resource, connection time and duration, transferred bytes, and client/software information. Stream-server operational logs may retain this information for service operation, troubleshooting and security. Logs are rotated as part of normal server operation.

The station also keeps aggregate listening counts and playback statistics. Its listener analytics are configured to suppress IP-based listener analytics. The iOS app does not request precise location, contacts, microphone recordings, or advertising identifiers. Passwords are sent directly to our authentication provider over an encrypted connection and are not stored by the app.

Advertising, tracking and service providers

Android version 1.0.0 and the current iOS release have no advertising or analytics SDK. Android 1.0.1 advertising and privacy controls are described below. Network information is processed by the hosting and radio services that deliver the app’s requested content. We do not sell personal information or share your local collection with advertisers. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the service.

Optional accounts, memberships and purchases

App versions with Discover and Account let you register or sign in with your verified email. We process your email address, account identifier, membership status, purchase records and access permissions to keep your website and app library synchronized. Authentication is provided by Supabase. Session credentials are saved in the device’s secure storage until you sign out. The app does not collect your payment-card details. Apple processes in-app purchases; Stripe processes website checkout. Purchase records and verified guest-checkout email addresses are used to restore access and prevent unauthorized downloads.

Public videos, photos and posts are delivered by the Golden Rama website and its hosting providers. Protected content uses short-lived access links. Files you explicitly save from a purchased release are temporarily downloaded for the device share menu. Members-only recordings have no download control.

Sharing and support

The Share button opens your device’s share menu. You choose whether and where to send a message. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include so we can respond. Your email provider and any destination you choose have their own privacy practices.

Your choices and deletion requests

Pause the radio to stop streaming. You can remove a bookmark by tapping its heart. Uninstalling the app removes its local data, subject to your device’s backup settings. If your app has an account, open Discover → Account → Delete my Golden Rama account. This starts permanent removal of the Golden Rama account and associated personal data within 7 days, with confirmation sent to your verified email. This is a manual fulfillment process and does not require a support call or email. Billing records legally required for tax, fraud prevention or disputes may be retained. Cancel Apple subscriptions in Apple’s subscription settings; Apple billing continues until cancelled. Website subscriptions are handled during deletion. Other Rich Row services are scoped separately. Older radio-only versions do not create accounts. For questions or requests about any support correspondence or service logs, email richrowrecords@gmail.com. We will handle requests subject to necessary security, operational and legal retention.

Audience and updates

The station may contain explicit lyrics. The app is not directed to children. We may update this policy as the service changes and will publish the revised date here. The separate artist website and any external services you choose to visit are governed by their own notices.

Advertising in Android version 1.0.1 and later

Updated November 14, 2026. Golden Rama Radio Android version 1.0.1 introduces a labeled banner on the Artist page beneath its biography. Availability depends on store review and rollout. This policy does not mean that ads are approved or serving. Android version 1.0.0 and the current iOS release retain their ad-free behavior.

Android version 1.0.1 and later use Google Mobile Ads to help fund the app. Google may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), advertising or device identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic/performance information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The SDK encrypts this information in transit. Non-personalized advertising still uses data for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

The Android integration checks regional consent requirements before initializing advertising. Open Artist → Ad privacy choices to review or withdraw consent where required, or retry a failed privacy check. Ads stay hidden if this check fails. The app requests non-personalized ads; limited ads may still be available after consent is declined where Google permits them. Declining consent, a failed consent check or an unavailable ad does not block the app's main features. Consult the version's release notes for availability. We do not provide saved songs, listening history, account passwords or private member content to Google as ad targeting data. Advertising does not require precise-location, camera or microphone access.

Google's own retention and user controls are described in Google's Privacy Policy and information for users of partner apps. Device advertising settings can also let you reset or delete an advertising identifier. The existing contact and deletion routes on this page continue to apply to information held by the app operator. Installing an update does not make your saved music collection public.
