Golden Rama Radio privacy

Golden Rama Radio is published by Dwayne Vidal / Rich Row Records. This policy covers the iPhone, iPad and Android radio app.

Updated November 13, 2026

Your collection stays on your device

No account is required. Saved song bookmarks and cached station history are stored locally. We do not upload your bookmarks or searches to a user profile. Saved songs are bookmarks, not music downloads.

Information used to deliver radio

When you listen or request song information and artwork, the radio service receives the network information necessary to respond. This can include your IP address, requested resource, connection time and duration, transferred bytes, and client/software information. Stream-server operational logs may retain this information for service operation, troubleshooting and security. Logs are rotated as part of normal server operation.

The station also keeps aggregate listening counts and playback statistics. Its listener analytics are configured to suppress IP-based listener analytics. We do not use the app to collect precise location, contacts, microphone recordings, photos, advertising identifiers, or account passwords.

Advertising, tracking and service providers

Existing releases without a labeled advertising banner have no advertising or analytics SDK and do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Supported Android advertising updates are described below. Network information is processed by the hosting and radio services that deliver the app’s requested content. We do not sell personal information or share your local collection with advertisers. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the service.

Sharing and support

The Share button opens your device’s share menu. You choose whether and where to send a message. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include so we can respond. Your email provider and any destination you choose have their own privacy practices.

Your choices and deletion requests

Pause the radio to stop streaming. You can remove a bookmark by tapping its heart. Uninstalling the app removes its local data, subject to your device’s backup settings. There is no app account to delete. For questions or requests about any support correspondence or service logs, email richrowrecords@gmail.com. We will handle requests subject to necessary security, operational and legal retention.

Audience and updates

The station may contain explicit lyrics. The app is not directed to children. We may update this policy as the service changes and will publish the revised date here. The separate artist website and any external services you choose to visit are governed by their own notices.

Advertising in supported Android updates

Updated November 14, 2026. Native advertising is being prepared for Android. This does not mean an advertising update is already available or that ads are serving. Earlier releases without a labeled banner retain their existing ad-free behavior. This section applies only to Android versions that include the banner and ad privacy controls; it does not enable native ads in the iOS app.

A supported Android version may use Google Mobile Ads to help fund the app. Google may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), advertising or device identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic/performance information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The SDK encrypts this information in transit. Non-personalized advertising still uses data for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

The intended integration checks regional consent requirements before initializing advertising and includes an Ad privacy choices control to review or withdraw consent where required. Declining consent, a failed consent check or an unavailable ad does not block the app's main features. Consult the version's release notes for availability. We do not provide calculator inputs, financial notebook records, account passwords or private member content to Google as ad targeting data. Advertising does not require precise-location, camera or microphone access.

Google's own retention and user controls are described in Google's Privacy Policy and information for users of partner apps. Device advertising settings can also let you reset or delete an advertising identifier. The existing contact and deletion routes on this page continue to apply to information held by the app operator. Installing an update does not make locally stored financial records public.
