Mi Nuh Know Yu Ft Alqual

Rest in peace, Alqual. Gone but not forgotten. Legends never die.

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Mi Nuh Know Yu brings Golden Rama and Alqual together around a blunt, memorable refusal to accept unwanted familiarity. The song turns an everyday encounter into a playful confrontation, using questions, character and attitude to establish a boundary. Its humor comes from how firmly that boundary is repeated.

The opening dialogue and later exchanges give the recording a theatrical quality. In the video, neighborhood streets, group performances and a wall of portraits keep the presentation close to people and public space. The result feels like a conversation that has become a performance, with personality doing as much work as the central refrain.

Alqual’s featured credit belongs visibly beside Golden Rama’s, as part of the identity of this collaboration. Returning to the song is also a way of keeping that contribution present for listeners discovering it now. Music gives a voice somewhere to remain: in the recording, in shared memories, and in the people who continue to press play.
