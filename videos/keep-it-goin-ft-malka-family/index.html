Keep It Goin Ft Malka Family

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Keep It Goin brings Golden Rama together with Malka Family in a collaboration built around movement and an invitation to stay in the moment. The repeated idea of keeping things going works naturally as a dance-floor call, giving the record a communal spirit that suits a room full of people responding together.

The video uses stark black-and-white portraits and graphic contrast to make performance the visual focus. With the background stripped back, faces, gestures and attitude carry the image. That direct presentation fits a song whose central appeal is participation: catch the refrain, find the movement, and become part of the atmosphere.

The Malka Family feature deserves a clear place in the title because collaboration is part of the record’s character. Within Golden Rama’s catalog, this release offers a welcome sense of shared celebration. Its staying power rests in a simple pleasure: a performance that gives the audience an easy way to join in.
