Everything Inna Place

Written by Dwayne Vidal — Author

Everything Inna Place gives Golden Rama’s faith a central place in the music. The song returns to gratitude, trust in Jah and the decision to put spiritual belief before the next step. Its message is especially meaningful when set against everyday difficulty: giving thanks is presented as a practice, not something reserved for easy times.

The waterfront setting gives the video room to breathe. Open sky, sea and direct performance create a quieter visual world than the night-time luxury of some of the other releases. That space suits a recording concerned with perspective, allowing the words of praise and reassurance to remain at the center.

This is an important part of Golden Rama’s range. Alongside the bold characters, party records and confrontational titles, there is a voice willing to express dependence, gratitude and hope. Everything Inna Place brings those qualities forward and offers listeners a simple point of connection: acknowledge the struggle, keep the faith, and be thankful for another day.
