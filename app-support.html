Golden Rama Radio support

Run di track. Feel di energy.

For help with the iPhone, iPad or Android app, email richrowrecords@gmail.com. Include your phone model, app version and what happened. Please do not send passwords or payment information.

Listen live

Open Radio and tap Listen live. Use Pause radio or your device’s media controls to stop. An internet connection is required, and your carrier’s data charges may apply. Playback can continue while using another app or when the screen is locked.

If playback stops

Check your internet connection, then tap Play to reconnect to the live station. Check the phone’s volume and audio output. The live station may occasionally be offline or interrupted. Pull down on the Radio page to refresh song information.

Your song discoveries

History shows tracks played by the station. Tap a heart to save a song bookmark, then open Saved to find it again. Search by song or artist. Bookmarks are stored on your device; they are not downloads or on-demand playback.

Sleep timer

Open the full player from the mini-player or the Radio page. Choose 15, 30 or 60 minutes to pause the radio later, or cancel the timer to continue listening.

Free access

The radio app requires no account, membership or payment. Some music may contain explicit lyrics.

Privacy

Read the Golden Rama Radio privacy policy. Privacy and deletion requests can be sent to the same support email.
