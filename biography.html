Golden Rama 440

Golden Rama 440 is a recording artist, producer, engineer and songwriter whose official artist site introduces the world of Bombshop Records. His Rich Row Records profile brings together the published releases, portraits and direct links associated with his music. It is a place to discover the artist’s work and continue into his wider creative catalog.

Biography

The catalog includes Kill Dem and Wah Gwaan, alongside recordings carrying the Golden Rama credit. His work also appears in Ask Anything, credited to The LAW and featuring Midrash Ramon and Golden Rama. Keeping these individual release credits visible helps listeners explore both his featured recordings and collaborative appearances.

Discover Golden Rama’s music here, then visit goldenrama440.com for his official artist experience, photographs, live sessions and updates. His site also provides the public routes for booking and contact with the artist team. This profile connects those destinations so listeners can move from a release to the artist behind it.
